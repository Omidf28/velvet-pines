Velvet Pines modifications
Normalized English keys, expanded slash-separated variants, removed duplicate
records, omitted unsupported keys and converted the profiles to TSV and SQLite.
The manifest records source hashes and omitted records. Source CEFR levels and
parts of speech are retained, including the source's missing part of speech.
Profiles describe headwords and parts of speech. Persian definitions are
provided by the separately attributed English-Persian dictionary.
